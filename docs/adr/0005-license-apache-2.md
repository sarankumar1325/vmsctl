# 0005. License: Apache-2.0

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-02

## Decision

hostlint is licensed under Apache-2.0.

## Why

It is the common choice for Go security tooling (e.g. Trivy, kube-bench), and its explicit
patent grant makes adoption easy for companies. AGPL would deter adoption; MIT lacks the patent
grant.
